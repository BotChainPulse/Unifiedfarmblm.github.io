Raising Local Chickens, Sharing What We Learn

Farm experience: Unified Farm BLM mainly raises local chickens. We do not currently raise broilers, commercial egg-laying chickens, or improved breeds. Information about these types is provided only for educational comparison, using cited agricultural guidance and current market examples. It does not describe the breeds we raise or the results achieved on our farm.

Our mission is to care for our flock responsibly, document real lessons from the farm and share useful guidance with other small-scale farmers.

How to Order & Delivery

1️⃣ Reach Out

Message us through the contact page or email info@unifiedfarmblm.com with what you need — local chickens, eggs, chicks or manure — plus your location and quantity.

2️⃣ Confirm & Book

We reply with current availability, collection or delivery options and the total price before you make arrangements.

3️⃣ Collect or Receive

Collection or delivery is arranged only after we confirm the item, quantity, destination and a suitable date. Farm visits are considered by prior arrangement and are not guaranteed.

Questions Farmers Ask Us

Do your chicks come vaccinated?

Vaccination status can vary by age and batch. We explain what has been given and what remains, and we recommend confirming a flock-specific programme with a qualified veterinary professional.

What is the minimum order for day-old chicks?

Minimum quantities depend on the current hatch and age of the birds. Contact us before planning your purchase.

How fresh are your eggs, really?

Egg availability varies with the local hens' laying cycle. Before buying, ask us to confirm the collection period, quantity available and recommended storage.

Can I visit the farm before buying?

Visits are considered only by prior arrangement and are not guaranteed. Contact us first so we can confirm whether a suitable date and time is available.

Do you deliver outside Mpigi?

Transport outside Mpigi may be discussed after we confirm the item, quantity, destination and current availability. Contact us before making any delivery plans.

I'm a complete beginner. Where do I start?

Start with a manageable number of local chickens, secure housing and a written budget. Our 90-day starter plan provides an educational example that you can adjust using current local prices.

Ready to Stock Your Farm?
