Privacy Policy

This policy describes the Unified Farm BLM poultry website at www.unifiedfarmblm.com, maintained by Reagan for our farm project in Mpigi, Uganda. Contact info@unifiedfarmblm.com with privacy questions.

Information you choose to send

Our contact links open your email app or WhatsApp. If you contact us, we receive the message and the contact details you share through that service. We use them to answer your enquiry, correct published information, or discuss availability and arrangements you request. Please do not send payment-card details, identity documents or other sensitive information through a general enquiry.

This website currently has no customer registration, online checkout, payment form, newsletter signup, public comments or survey form. Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing messages.

Calculators and record-sheet downloads

The feed, egg and broiler calculators calculate in your browser. Their code does not send the figures you enter to a farm database. CSV record sheets download without registration; entering information into your downloaded copy does not upload it to this website. Keep completed records private and back them up yourself.

Hosting and third-party connections

GitHub Pages hosts this website. Hosting and content-delivery providers can receive technical request information, such as an IP address, requested page, browser information and request time, when they deliver a page or file. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Some pages load Google AdSense code. The contact page also loads the QRCode.js library from cdnjs to display a WhatsApp QR code. Requests to third-party providers can disclose technical browser and connection information to them. A district map opens on Google Maps only when you follow its link. Email, WhatsApp, Google Maps and other external sites operate under their own privacy policies once you use them.

Google advertising and cookies

As of the review date above, the site has not been approved for AdSense; inclusion of Google's code does not mean advertising is active. Google's privacy policy applies to its services and code: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner sites.

When Google ads are served, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on earlier visits to this website or other websites. Google's advertising cookies allow Google and its partners to serve personalised ads based on visits to this and other sites. Other participating advertising vendors may also use cookies.

You can manage personalised Google advertising at My Ad Center and find participating third-party advertising choices at AboutAds choices. Browser settings can also control stored cookies. These controls are separate from any consent choices presented by an advertising provider.

How enquiries are handled

We use enquiry details for the conversation or arrangement you requested. Information needed for an agreed collection or delivery may be shared with the person arranging it. We do not sell enquiry contact lists. We retain correspondence while it is needed for the enquiry, agreed transaction, a dispute or a record we are required to keep; contact us to ask what we hold and request correction or deletion where applicable.

Email, messaging, hosting and advertising providers may process information outside Uganda. Their policies explain their own processing and safeguards. Avoid sending information you are not comfortable sharing through the chosen service.

Your questions and choices

Write to info@unifiedfarmblm.com to ask about your enquiry information, request a correction or ask for deletion. Do not include unnecessary sensitive documents. We may need enough information to identify the relevant conversation. Requests concerning a third-party account or its cookies should also be directed to that provider.

Changes

We update this page when the site's features or data practices change and show the review date above. This policy concerns the farm website; a separate application may have its own privacy policy.
