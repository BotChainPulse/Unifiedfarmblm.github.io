How to use the QR Code Generator

A QR code is a square barcode that a phone camera can scan to open or display information quickly. To create one here, enter the website address, phone number, message or other text you want to share. Choose an image size, tap Generate QR code, test the result with your phone camera, and then download the PNG if it works as expected.

For a website, include the complete address beginning with https://. For plain text, keep the message concise so the finished code remains easy to scan. If you are printing a code on packaging, posters or signs, use one of the larger image sizes and keep enough white space around the code.

What can a QR code contain?

You can encode a website link, product page, farm information page, short message, phone number, email address or another piece of text. A QR code simply stores the information you enter; what happens after scanning depends on the phone and the type of content.

Useful farm and business ideas

QR codes can help connect printed materials to online information. A farmer or small business can place one on an egg tray label, product leaflet, price list, market stall sign, event poster or business card so customers can quickly reach a website, contact page or product information.

Make your QR code easy to scan

Keep strong contrast between the dark and light squares, avoid stretching the downloaded image, and do not place text or logos over the code unless you have tested the result carefully. Before printing many copies, scan a sample from several distances and on more than one phone if possible.

Important tips before you print or share

  • Test the destination first: make sure the website or text is correct before printing the QR code.
  • Remember that this is a static QR code: the encoded information is built into the image. If you later change a website address, you normally need to generate a new QR code.
  • Use a clear print size: very small or blurry codes can be difficult for phone cameras to read.
  • Keep the surrounding space clear: the white margin around a QR code helps scanners recognise its edges.
  • Avoid sensitive information: anyone who receives or photographs the QR code may be able to read the encoded content.

Privacy and safety

This generator creates the QR code in your browser. The text you type is not intentionally sent to Unified Farm BLM for storage. Even so, a downloaded QR code should be treated like any other public label or image: do not put passwords, private account details, secret keys or other confidential information inside it. When scanning QR codes from other people, check the destination shown by your phone before opening unfamiliar links.
