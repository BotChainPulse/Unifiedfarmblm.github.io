Why calculate before you expand?

Egg income can look healthy while feed, transport, cracked eggs and labour quietly consume the margin. A useful budget starts with your actual laying rate and local prices—not an advertised profit figure. Recalculate whenever feed or tray prices change, and compare the estimate with your farm records at the end of each month.

Planning note: These tools provide estimates, not guaranteed returns. Weather, flock health, mortality, buyer demand and price changes affect real results. Use current quotations from your suppliers and buyers.
