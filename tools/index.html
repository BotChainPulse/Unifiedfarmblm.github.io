Why calculate before you expand?

Poultry income can look healthy while feed, transport, mortality, cracked eggs and labour quietly consume the margin. A useful budget starts with your actual flock records and current local prices—not an advertised profit figure. Recalculate whenever feed or selling prices change, and compare each estimate with your real results at the end of the cycle.

These tools are designed to make the assumptions visible. You can change production rate, mortality, feed intake, selling price and operating costs instead of relying on one fixed example that may not match your farm.

Planning note: These tools provide estimates, not guaranteed returns. Weather, flock health, mortality, buyer demand and price changes affect real results. Use current quotations from your suppliers and buyers and seek qualified veterinary or nutrition advice when needed.
