Local Chicks in Mpigi

Unified Farm BLM mainly raises local chickens. Ask about the age, quantity, vaccination information and current availability of local chicks before making purchase or transport plans.

This is a guide figure. Confirm the current price and availability before ordering.

Confirm the age

Available local chicks may be at different stages, so ask for their age and expected collection date.

Collection or delivery

Collect in Mpigi or ask about transport to Wakiso, Kampala and other districts for larger orders.

What to confirm

  • The chick age and quantity available.
  • Vaccinations already given and any follow-up responsibility.
  • The feeding routine used before collection.
  • Collection or delivery arrangements.

Prepare before collection: brooder heat, clean water, suitable feed and secure housing must be ready before the birds arrive.

Frequently asked questions

Are the chicks vaccinated?

Vaccination status varies by age and batch. We share the available information and recommend confirming the follow-up programme with a qualified veterinary professional.

Is availability guaranteed?

No. Confirm the age, preferred date and quantity before making plans.

Can beginners order?

Yes. Tell us about your housing and flock goal so we can point out the essentials to prepare before arrival.

Ready to order or ask a question?

Tell us what you need, the quantity and your location. We confirm current availability, collection or delivery arrangements before you pay.
