Frequently asked questions

How much does consultation cost?

Contact us with the topic and level of support you need. We confirm the rate before booking.

Can you diagnose sick birds?

We can share general farm-management guidance, but illness diagnosis and treatment should be handled by a qualified veterinary professional.

Is this useful before I buy chicks?

Yes. Planning housing, feed, health schedules and cash flow before purchasing birds reduces avoidable losses.

Ready to order or ask a question?

Tell us what you need, the quantity and your location. We confirm current availability, collection or delivery arrangements before you pay.
