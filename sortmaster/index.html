How to Play

  1. Tap any tube to pick up its top color.
  2. Tap another tube to pour — colors only stack on matching colors (or into empty tubes).
  3. Sort every color into its own tube to clear the level. 🎉
  4. Stuck? Use Undo, restart, or add an extra tube!
