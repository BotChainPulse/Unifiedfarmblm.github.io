Privacy Policy — Sort Master

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Unified Farm BLM ("we", "our") operates the Sort Master mobile application and the sortmaster web game (the "Game"). This page explains what data is — and is not — collected when you play.

1. Data we do NOT collect

Sort Master requires no account, no sign-up, and collects no personal information. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, files, or precise location. Your game progress is stored only on your own device and never leaves it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game displays ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location
  • Device model, OS version, and ad interaction data

This data is collected by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and Google's advertising terms. You can limit ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Delete/reset advertising ID or opt out of personalization).

3. Children's privacy

The Game is a general-audience puzzle and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. No personal information is required to play.

4. Data security & retention

Since we collect no personal data, we retain none. Game progress lives in your device's local storage and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

5. Changes

We may update this policy; the current version is always available at this URL.
