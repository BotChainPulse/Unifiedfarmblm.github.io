Our Story

A growing poultry project in Mpigi, Uganda, focused on local chickens and honest lessons from the farm.

How It Started

Unified Farm BLM began as a small poultry project in Mpigi, Uganda. Like many small farming operations, we continue learning through practical experience and careful observation.

Farm experience: Unified Farm BLM mainly raises local chickens. We do not currently raise broilers, commercial egg-laying chickens, or improved breeds. Information about these types is provided only for educational comparison, using cited agricultural guidance and current market examples. It does not describe the breeds we raise or the results achieved on our farm.

Farm Experience

Content describing our farm focuses on the local chickens we actually raise. Educational comparisons do not describe the breeds we keep or results achieved on our farm.

Where We Are Today

Today, Unified Farm BLM raises local chickens and shares practical poultry guidance from Mpigi. We continue to grow carefully, using our records rather than presenting changing flock or production figures as permanent claims.

We are not a commercial mega-farm. We are a small, working poultry project documenting what succeeds, what needs improvement and what other farmers should verify for their own conditions.

Our expertise areas:

  • Local-chicken management: We focus on practical housing, feeding, natural brooding and flock observation for local chickens.
  • Vaccination and biosecurity: We use written schedules and encourage farmers to confirm flock-specific programmes with a qualified veterinary professional.
  • Nutrition and feed management: We compare feed use with production records and caution beginners against mixing rations without qualified guidance.
  • Market access and pricing: We share practical lessons from selling locally while making clear that prices vary by location, season and buyer.
  • Farmer learning: Our guides combine local experience with links to recognised animal-health and agricultural sources.

Why We Do This

We chose poultry farming because local chickens can be raised gradually by households and small farms. Results depend on breed, health, housing, feed, weather and market conditions.

Our aim is to build the farm carefully, keep more reliable records and share useful lessons with other small poultry keepers. Products and practical support are offered only when current availability and our capacity allow.

What We Believe

🌱 Start Small, Learn Fast

Begin with a flock you can house, feed and monitor properly. Learn the daily routine, keep records and expand only when the current flock is manageable.

💉 Health Before Profit

We treat hygiene, biosecurity and health records as priorities. Vaccination decisions depend on each bird's age, source and previous programme, and should be confirmed with a qualified veterinary professional.

🤝 Community Over Competition

We share what we know. When another farmer in Mpigi succeeds, we all benefit. The demand for eggs in Uganda is bigger than any single farm can meet.

🎯 Quality Over Quantity

We favour careful handling, honest availability information and consistent standards over exaggerated production claims.

Why Trust Our Advice

We write about our field experience, but experience alone is not enough for technical or animal-health advice. We separate observations from general guidance, show assumptions behind calculations, and link readers to recognised sources.

  • Real experience: We explain what we observed and avoid presenting one farm's result as a guarantee.
  • Local context: We farm in the same conditions as our readers. We deal with the same feed shortages, the same rainy seasons, the same market fluctuations, and the same veterinary challenges.
  • Source checking: We consult recognised agricultural and animal-health resources and encourage veterinary confirmation for flock-specific decisions.
  • Transparency: Prices and budgets are examples that readers should replace with current local quotations.
  • Corrections: Readers can report errors, and confirmed errors are corrected openly.
