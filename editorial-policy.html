Editorial and Corrections Policy

Unified Farm BLM publishes practical poultry guidance for small-scale farmers in Uganda. Our purpose is to share usable field lessons while clearly separating farm experience, illustrative calculations and professional veterinary guidance.

How we prepare a guide

  • Field context: Reagan Mitanda writes from experience operating a small poultry project in Mpigi and speaking with local farmers and buyers.
  • Source checking: Animal-health claims are checked against recognised sources such as the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO) and the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH).
  • Local verification: Feed prices, chick prices and selling prices are examples, not fixed market quotations. Farmers should confirm current prices with suppliers and buyers.
  • Updates: We revise articles when guidance, market conditions or our own field experience changes materially. A changed date should reflect a meaningful revision.
  • Corrections: Readers can report an error to info@unifiedfarmblm.com. We review the evidence and correct confirmed errors.

Health and medicine

Important: Our articles are educational and cannot diagnose a flock. Disease signs often overlap. Contact a qualified veterinary professional or district veterinary officer before treating birds. Use vaccines and veterinary medicines according to the product label and professional advice, including the stated withdrawal period. Do not use antibiotics for viral disease or as routine growth promoters.

Financial examples

Budgets and profit illustrations show how to calculate a decision. They are not earnings promises. Feed prices, mortality, laying rates, transport, labour and selling prices vary by farm and season. Our free calculators let readers replace examples with their own figures.
